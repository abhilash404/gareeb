Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This is a small browser game. This page explains what it stores about you when you play.

Signing in

You sign in with your Google account through Supabase, our login provider. The game never sees or stores your Google password. From Google we receive only your basic profile (name, email address and an account id), which Supabase keeps so you can sign in again.

What the game stores

  • Your character: the username you choose, race and class.
  • Your progress: inventory and where you were in the world.
  • Changes you make to the shared world.

Your username and character are visible to other players. Your email address is not.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. The game keeps your login session in your browser so you stay signed in.

Deleting your data

Ask through the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for this game, and your account and character will be deleted.
